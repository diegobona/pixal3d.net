FAQ

Can I turn any image into a 3D model?

Pixal3D works best when the image has one clear subject. Complex scenes usually need cropping or cleanup first.

Is the result ready for production?

Treat the first result as a generated 3D draft. Review topology, scale, and texture details before production use.

Where do I start?

Use the generator on the home page, then iterate with cleaner references if the first shape is not clear enough.
