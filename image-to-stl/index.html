FAQ

Can I print the generated model immediately?

Always inspect and repair a generated mesh before printing. STL files often need cleanup, scaling, and slicing checks.

What images work best for STL preparation?

Simple objects, clear silhouettes, and references without heavy occlusion usually make better starting points.

Is this only for 3D printing?

No. The same image-to-3D process can also support visualization, concept modeling, and asset planning.
